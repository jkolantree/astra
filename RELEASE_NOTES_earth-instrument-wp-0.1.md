## What this paper explores

An illustrated systems synthesis about plate boundaries, geological memory, preservation filters, origins, and tests that can distinguish plausible histories from unsupported ones. Here, *instrument* means a natural coupled system that stores, transforms, filters, and reveals evidence—not a claim that Earth was engineered.

The evidence labels are the paper author's classifications, not peer review, scientific consensus, or institutional endorsement. Gaps in geological or archaeological preservation do not provide positive evidence for a particular civilization or technology.
