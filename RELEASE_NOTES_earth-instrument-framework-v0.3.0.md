## Scientific and accessibility boundaries

The geological, biological, archaeological, and arithmetic cases are calibration examples; they do not establish one shared hidden mechanism or provide empirical validation of SPPT/ASTRA. Synthetic benchmarks are not planetary measurements. Finite searches and prime reductions are bounded evidence, not unrestricted proofs. The higher-dimensional arithmetic example does not settle the open two-dimensional case.

The PDFs are searchable, tagged, and font-embedded, but are not claimed as PDF/UA-conformant. Mathematical semantics and some fixed-layout relationships remain incomplete, and the PDF audit form is static. The reflowable Pages companions are the primary accessible entry points.
