Ground reading

Earth can be modeled simultaneously as reactor, archive, censor, and instrument. Here, instrument means a natural coupled system that stores, transforms, filters, and reveals evidence. Censor means that preservation, exposure, sampling, and recognition remove or distort parts of the record; it does not imply consciousness, agency, or design.

A seam can change the physical future and can change what an observer is able to infer. Those effects are different. ASTRA v0.3.0 asks researchers to measure them separately and then prevents a true local result from being promoted into a false global conclusion.

Ground rule

State the atomic claim, observer, comparator, uncertainty model, system boundary, alternatives, chronology, and demotion condition before promotion. A shared analytical pattern does not establish a shared physical mechanism or history.

The dual-rent boundary criterion

Let Γ denote the seam being studied and Γ0 a declared reference seam or protocol.

Dynamical rent: does the seam change reachable futures?

Rdyn (Γ)= d( P(Y|do(Γ)), P(Y|do(Γ0)) )

The distance function d must be declared. It may compare outcome distributions, reachable states, or another operational quantity appropriate to the domain.

Epistemic rent: does the seam change causal discrimination?

Repi (Γ,π)= I(K;D|Γ,π) − I(K;D|Γ0,π)

This asks whether the seam and observation protocol make candidate causes easier or harder to distinguish. The observer, candidates, data, protocol, and information measure must all be named.

Four operational seam categories
Category Operational meaning
Amplifying instrument The physical future changes, and discrimination among candidate causes improves.
Diagnostic seam The physical future does not change, but discrimination among candidate causes improves.
Stateful but masking The physical future changes, but discrimination among candidate causes does not improve.
Cosmetic boundary Neither the physical future nor discrimination among candidate causes changes.

Promotion requires a predeclared intervention, observation protocol, negative control, or exact certificate that pays predictive rent. These text labels carry the meaning; color is not required.

Seven 2026 calibration cases

These studies are independent laboratories for boundaries, propagation paths, and observation errors. They do not provide evidence for one hidden mechanism and do not directly answer the historical origin of humanity.

  1. Localized IL-10 immunomodulation

    Seam lesson
    A localized intervention can change implant-host immune fate; identity and integration are relational, not genome-only.
    Boundary
    Preclinical rodent and nonhuman-primate work does not establish human efficacy.
    Primary source
    Kim and colleagues, Science Advances (2026).

  2. Magnetar polarization

    Seam lesson
    Propagation can add a diagnostic field signature; the received signal is not identical to the emitted state.
    Boundary
    The vacuum-birefringence interpretation is model-dependent strong inference, not geometry-free proof.
    Primary source
    Stewart and colleagues, Nature (2026).

  3. Collisionless phase mixing

    Seam lesson
    A coherent trajectory can appear as decorrelated time structure; the sampling path belongs in the forward model.
    Boundary
    The work supplies an analytical mechanism, not a prevalence estimate for historical misclassification.
    Primary source
    Osmane and colleagues, Physical Review Research (2026).

  4. Possible basal nitrogen flow on Pluto

    Seam lesson
    A material phase boundary may enable transient flow; the actual nitrogen phase diagram matters more than a water analogy.
    Boundary
    This is a morphology- and physics-based hypothesis, not a direct observation of flow.
    Primary source
    Stern and colleagues, The Planetary Science Journal (2026).

  5. Nanoparticle measurement error

    Seam lesson
    An errors-in-variables model can recover a relationship attenuated by predictor error; weak observed correlation is not automatically a strong physical constraint.
    Boundary
    The correction must match the measurement process and uncertainty model.
    Primary source
    Pintar and colleagues, ACS Nano (2026).

  6. Silver-exchanged zeolite

    Seam lesson
    Pore geometry can amplify isotope differences; anomalies may encode selective fractionation.
    Boundary
    The reported result is cryogenic laboratory separation, not a completed industrial lifecycle.
    Primary source
    Becker and colleagues, Nature Communications (2026).

  7. Chloroplast myoglobin

    Seam lesson
    Inherited machinery can execute a foreign sequence incompletely; expression is not ecological autonomy.
    Boundary
    Partial heme occupancy means functional and commercial equivalence remain open.
    Primary source
    Groff and colleagues, Frontiers in Plant Science (2026).

Local truth, global failure

A 2026 mathematical result supplies an exact calibration case. An explicit polynomial map from three-dimensional complex space to itself has constant Jacobian determinant -2, yet three distinct rational inputs share one output:

F(0, 0, -1/4) = F(1, -3/2, 13/2) = F(-1, 3/2, 13/2) = (-1/4, 0, 0).

The local derivative certificate is exact, but global injectivity fails because complete fibers and behavior at infinity are missing. The result gives counterexamples in dimensions three and higher by adjoining identity coordinates. The two-dimensional Jacobian conjecture remains open.

Recent-result boundary

This is extraordinary, very recent mathematics. ASTRA did not originate the counterexample and its executable replay is not a substitute for independent mathematical review. The cited 2026 note and preprints were not peer reviewed at the release date.

Use the following certificate stack before promoting any local result:

  1. Local response: derivative, assay, mode, morphology, or bounded fit.
  2. Complete fibers: enumerate preimages and viable alternatives.
  3. Closure and infinity: check omitted reservoirs, escape paths, and properness.
  4. Arithmetic reductions: identify good and bad primes, valuations, and lifting limits.
  5. Formal replay: bind the exact object, identities, deterministic test, hashes, and scope.

Prime channels and finite differences

For a prime p and integer a, define a prime-difference expression and the Artin-Schreier polynomial:

Ep(a)= ap− (a−1)p−1 , Ap(t)= tp−t

The exact identity is:

Ep(a)= Ap(a)− Ap(a−1)

In characteristic p, the derivative of Ap is the nonzero constant -1, while translation by an element of the finite field leaves the value unchanged. Local nonsingularity therefore coexists with p-to-one fibers.

The package also records divisibility and valuation restrictions. Its exact search found no perfect-power hit only in the frozen domain:

p in {5, 11, 17, 23, 29, 41, 47}; 2 <= a <= 5000.

That is an established finite computation within its bounds. It is not a proof of the unrestricted perfect-power claim.

Scientific and release boundaries

  • The framework is a methodological working paper, not a discovered ancient technology, non-terrestrial origin of humans, or proof of intentional planetary design.
  • The seven empirical studies retain their original sample, model, species, scale, and uncertainty limits.
  • Prime reductions are arithmetic observation channels, not independent statistical replications and not automatic lifts to characteristic zero.
  • The higher-dimensional Jacobian result does not settle the plane case.
  • The synthetic benchmarks fit no planet, excavation, monument, genome, historical text, or mission dataset.
  • The package's ASTRA-Layers and FOG representations are structured mirrors with immutable identifiers, not byte-identical mounted originals.
  • The restored SPPT helper and original-scope tests are functional reconstructions, not byte-identical original files.
