Accessibility statement

The compact ground-reading and audit PDFs are tagged, text based, searchable, and built with embedded fonts. They are not claimed to conform to PDF/UA or to be fully accessible. Known limitations include very small fixed-layout text, insufficient contrast in some branded and diagram text, incomplete semantic heading and table structure, non-semantic mathematics, and a print-only rather than interactive audit form.

These browser pages are the primary reflowable path for the compact public reading and worksheet. They use semantic headings, native controls, described tables, accessible mathematical text, strong contrast, keyboard focus, and print styles. They are not a line-by-line HTML transcription of the 171-page paper.

The supplied verification report is an internal package and release report. Its successful checks establish stated integrity, replay, source-metadata, and rendering properties within their declared scope; they are not independent peer review, external scientific endorsement, or replication of every cited experiment.

Report a reading or accessibility problem after removing secrets, private paths, account details, and non-public data.

What v0.3.0 adds

Dual rent

It separates a seam's effect on reachable physical futures from its effect on what an observer can distinguish.

Equifinality triad

It distinguishes different histories reaching one state, different states becoming one record, and boundaries that amplify a diagnostic difference.

Local-to-global certificates

It asks whether a local response has complete fibers, closure at infinity, arithmetic support, and deterministic replay before promotion to a global claim.

Arithmetic seam laboratory

It replays an explicit three-variable Keller map, prime reductions, an Artin-Schreier finite-difference identity, and a strictly bounded perfect-power search.

Guardrail: shared inverse-problem structure does not establish one shared hidden physical mechanism. The seven scientific cases are calibration cases, not evidence of directed seeding, a separate insertion of humans, a completed off-world biosphere, or an ancient industrial system.
