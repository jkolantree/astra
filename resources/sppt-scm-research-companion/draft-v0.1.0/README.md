# SPPT / SCM research companion — unpromoted draft

This collection preserves the 32 exact payload files supplied in the three
approved research and visual bundles of 5 October 2026. It adds a static explorer
with accessibility features and an explicit review record. Stable SPPT/ASTRA v1.0.7, the
core claim matrix, historical packages and production Pages retain their
independent authority.

The package contains the 35-page public research synthesis in PDF and DOCX,
the 12-page atlas PDF, six scientific figures in PNG/SVG, two prescribed
animations in GIF/MP4 with still posters, three original AI-generated images,
and their generator, captions, source links, catalog and provenance.
Incoming ZIP containers and duplicated split-bundle wrapper files are not
redistributed. Original payload bytes, filenames and C2PA chunks are retained.
No older archive, source paper, personal photograph or private conversation is
included. Earlier research remains linked at its original repository location.

## Status and limits

This is a research companion, not a stable science release or empirical
validation. Figure statuses distinguish proposed observation schematics,
conditional model calculations, analytic geometry, prescribed motion and
AI-generated speculative illustration. Sources are cited rather than copied.
The original research report retains its source-access limitations.

The supplied generator was replayed in an isolated directory. Its conditional
mathematical checks passed; that is not evidence of a physical mechanism.
The available replay used Matplotlib 3.11.1 instead of the delivered build’s
3.10.8. Its 18 media outputs were not byte-identical. No replay output replaces
the approved media, and byte-for-byte build reproduction is not claimed.
Explorer checks cover exact pinned samples, independent matrix-exponential
comparisons, analytic geometry and JavaScript callbacks. They are not browser
rendering or accessibility certification. The executor's attempted browser
navigation was blocked by administrator policy; no alternate browser route
was attempted. The owner subsequently inspected the delivered preview on their
own device and accepted its general appearance. Missing images were resolved
by extracting both ZIPs into the same folder; no image-path change was needed.
Specialist test coverage remains separately recorded as not tested.

The prepared Pages integration adds `/explore/` as an entry point to this
versioned draft namespace. Admission v2 pins the complete companion and retains
the existing frozen release routes. The manual deployment workflow requires an
exact-byte integrity, manual visual acceptance and final publication approval.
Visual acceptance and owner publication authorization are recorded. Successful
exact-head CI and the owner’s merge remain separate requirements.
Preparing or assembling this draft does not deploy it.
