## M1 corrections admitted into this candidate

1. **Weak-cut wording.** Slow relaxation follows from weak cut conductance relative to the aggregate capacities on both sides under the theorem's hypotheses. Low capacity alone does not imply a slow mode.
2. **Dynamic-arrest logic.** Vanishing logarithmic slope is neither necessary nor sufficient for bounded or statistically stationary cluster scale. The repair requires a preregistered boundedness/stationarity assessment together with persistent resolved turnover and keeps the definition proposed-only.
3. **Coverage-language correction.** The maintenance record distinguishes structural path support from exact locators and does not claim external entailment re-verification, complete source-byte provenance, or full command/runtime/run-ID coverage.
