# ASTRA proposed methods draft: mode-resolved active-support audit

Status: unpromoted default-branch research draft; not peer reviewed and not a
release.

This is a separately named ASTRA methods perspective. It does not revise the
Solar-Planetary Phase-Partition Theory (SPPT) equations, the v1.0.7 claim
matrix or its release identity, any immutable tag or asset, the Earth Is the
Instrument line, or the Sector-Complete Instrument alpha. It has no GitHub Release, Pages route,
Zenodo record, DOI, or release identity.

## Why this draft exists

The attached synthesis compares three heterogeneous reports. Their scientific
mechanisms are not the same, but each makes a similar experimental-design
warning: total input, average power, nominal inventory, or a component label may
not identify the support that is active under a particular mode and waveform.
This draft turns that warning into an auditable ASTRA bookkeeping layer.

The draft asks:

1. Which mode is being operated?
2. What support in space, time, frequency, or geometry is plausibly active?
3. What measurement actually observes the response?
4. What intervention would distinguish the proposed mechanism from a confounder?
5. What residue or held-out prediction would remain after the intervention?

The answer is intentionally narrower than a new physical law. Active support is
a proposed reporting object, not a universal field, a dark-matter detector, or
a substitute for a constitutive bridge.

## Evidence boundary

- The flying-focus study is a version-of-record experiment: a 7 mm
  laser-plasma accelerator used a programmable focus and reported a maximum
  electron energy of 396 +/- 14 MeV near a density of 5 x 10^18 cm^-3. Its
  100 GeV, sub-metre scaling is a projection, not an achieved beam.
- The pied-flycatcher study is a behavioral result in Emlen funnels. It
  compares continuous and pulsed radiofrequency conditions and constrains one
  direct cryptochrome-decoherence explanation. It does not locate a receptor or
  prove an induction detector.
- The silver/perovskite item remains a source-summary lead. The exact primary
  article, DOI, full parameters, and rights were not resolved in this audit;
  its chemistry claims therefore remain source_asserted or deferred.
- The common statement that mode and active support matter is a methodological
  inference. It is not evidence for one shared mechanism.

## Relationship to SPPT and dark matter

SPPT/ASTRA v1.0.7 remains the current physical core: a thermodynamically constrained hybrid
phase-reservoir graph with declared units, transport, boundary conditions,
topology guards, and promotion by held-out prediction. This draft adds no
planetary reservoir law and no dark-matter claim. The immutable v1.0.6 release
remains the historical core baseline used by this audit's frozen source record.

Any later adapter for a dark-matter search would need a declared interaction
operator, coupling normalization, source and abundance model, coherence
assumptions, detector transfer function in physical units, nuisance model,
multi-sector nulls, and a preregistered analysis. A sensitivity number or a
structural analogy cannot be relabeled as a dark-matter observation,
abundance estimate, origin story, or purpose.

## Reproduction and promotion boundary

This draft is documentation and ledger work only. It contains no executable
scientific reproduction and does not claim independent replication. Before a
future promotion, freeze a new candidate tree, resolve the SNU primary source,
add claim-local locators and hashes, define units, run preregistered controls,
and create a separate manifest, rights map, citation record, and release
identity. A future release must not mutate v1.0.6 or reuse its identity.
