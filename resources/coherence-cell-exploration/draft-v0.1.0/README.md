# ASTRA coherence-cell exploration: wave packets, stress flux, and falsifiers

Status: repository-visible, unpromoted supplemental research draft. This is not
a release, not peer reviewed, and not a new SPPT or ASTRA core claim.

This note develops a cautious research program from the supplied “foam,
pressure, crest, and code” proposal. It preserves the useful intuition while
separating four different things that are often conflated:

1. an observed physical result;
2. the standard equation that currently explains it;
3. a proposed analogy or additional term; and
4. a measurement that could falsify the proposal.

The proposed method is called **AEOF**: Analogy -> Equation -> Observable ->
Falsifier. It is an ASTRA research-design contribution, not a claim that the
universe has been shown to be an ether, cellular automaton, static tired-light
system, or black-hole reset machine.

## Scope

This draft develops five bounded lines of work:

- replace “electron pulsing” with measurable coherent Rydberg wave-packet
  dynamics;
- translate “pressure” and “interference” into stress tensors, energy
  gradients, and domain-specific constitutive laws;
- compare collapse and release across BECs, white dwarfs, magnetic systems,
  AGN feedback, and relativistic collapse without asserting one mechanism;
- parameterize, rather than assume, a possible discrete-substrate correction;
- build a joint redshift discriminator instead of treating tired light as an
  established alternative.

The cosmological, ether, photon-crest, and black-hole-reset interpretations
remain proposed or rejected at the claim level. No third-party article bytes,
figures, raw datasets, or publisher layouts are included.

## Evidence boundary

The strongest empirical bridge is prepared Rydberg wave-packet dynamics. A
stationary hydrogen eigenstate does not contain a moving electron shell, while
a coherent superposition can have time-dependent radial probability and
collapse/revival. That distinction is central to the note.

The proposed stress-flux and coherence-support-release structures are methods
hypotheses. They become physics only if they supply unit-consistent equations,
independent replay, and a prediction that beats an appropriate standard-model
baseline on held-out data.
