## What the three new calibration cases add

The three September 2026 articles were traced to their primary records and used
only where their inference structure transfers.

1. A prescribed gravitational pulse can restore its macroscopic endpoint while
   a quantum state retains path-dependent Bogoliubov occupation. This is a
   conventional state-memory null, not evidence for a persistent medium.
2. Microwave-assisted SnO2 crystallization shows that a faster observed rate
   need not mean a lower fitted barrier. Strong Arrhenius barrier-prefactor
   compensation and unresolved local temperature make mechanism labels
   non-unique.
3. 450P/LONEOS separates a timed orbital perturbation, latent thermal history,
   phase conversion, and delayed gas/dust observations. The causal chain is
   informative precisely because every arrow is model-assisted.

Together they motivate the rule: do not name a residual until endpoint
restoration, parameter compensation, latent history, and the observation
operator have each been tested.
